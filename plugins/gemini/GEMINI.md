# Does It ARM

Does It ARM is a public, read-only catalog of reported Apple Silicon compatibility. Results include source listing links when available. A missing listing means compatibility is unknown in this catalog.

- `search_apps` finds Mac apps, games, and Homebrew formulae by name, alias, or bundle ID. `get_app` reads a listing, including version and architecture evidence when available.
- `check_apps` compares up to 50 names. `check_inventory` matches Mac app inventory against catalog evidence and reports unknowns when version-specific evidence is missing.
- `list_categories` and `list_by_category` browse the catalog. `recent_updates` shows recently updated app listings. `list_devices` lists Apple Silicon Mac models.
